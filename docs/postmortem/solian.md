## Biggest challenge
My biggest challenge was getting the project to actually run on my computer. At first the project was running only on one machine at a time. One person would fix the gradle on their machine, it would work and then we would push something over and it would all be broken again. After a good while of troubleshooting we realized that our gradle files were in our git ignores. This was a huge realization but after we took it out of all our git ignores it worked moving forward.

## Most valuable thing I learned
The most valuable thing I learned was that sometimes the solution to problems are simple ones. Throughout our project we had large goals and at first they seemed more complicated than they actually were. For example our biggest challenge where the gradle files weren't syncing I thought it had to do something with our OS. 2 of our teammates were using linux machines so I was under the impression that maybe that was why our gradle files weren't working. Later when we realized it was something so simple as the git ignore it made me realize that sometimes you just need to take a step back and think about the issue before trying to solve it.
